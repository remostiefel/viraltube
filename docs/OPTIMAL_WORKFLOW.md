# ⚡ CORTEX OPTIMAL CONSTRUCT
> **The Definitive Workflow: From Trend Radar to Viral Asset**

This document outlines the precision workflow for the **Neuro-Code Optimizer (CORTEX)**. Follow these steps to maximize efficiency and ensuring every piece of content uses the full power of the system.

---

## 🟢 PHASE 1: DISCOVERY (Trend Radar)
**Goal:** Identifying the "Viral Spark".

1.  **Navigate:** Go to **Notebook** → **Trend Radar**.
2.  **Scan:** Review the incoming signals. Look for **"Outliers"** (High Viral Score, Low Competition).
3.  **Select:** Click the **`+` (Plus)** button on a topic.
    *   *System Action:* Topic is added to your personal "Idea List".
    *   *Visual:* Card turns green / Checkmark appears.

---

## 🟡 PHASE 2: INCUBATION (Notebook)
**Goal:** Refining the raw idea into a concrete concept.

1.  **Navigate:** Go to **Notebook** → **Topics** tab.
2.  **Review Idea:** Find your new topic in the **"Idea"** column.
3.  **Refine (Optional):** Click the **Pencil Icon** to tweak the title or add personal notes (e.g., "Use 12s Nano format for this").
4.  **Deep Dive:** If uncertain, click the **Brain Icon** to run a quick specific scan on this keyword.

---

## 🔴 PHASE 3: THE ARCHITECT (Genesis)
**Goal:** Transforming the concept into a production-ready script.

1.  **Trigger:** Click the **⚡ (Zap/Lightning)** icon on the topic card.
    *   *Action:* This sends the topic to the **Script Forge**.
2.  **Configure:** In the Script Forge (Architect):
    *   **Format:** Select **`12s-Short (Nano)`** (or appropriate format).
    *   **Tone:** Ensure "Neuro-Code" or your specific persona is active.
    *   **Loop Logic:** Verify `Loop: ON` if using the Nano format.
3.  **Generations:** Click **"Generate Blueprint"**.
    *   *System Action:* CORTEX consults `viral-wisdom` (Self-Learning) and generates the script using the active Format Profile.

---

## 🔵 PHASE 4: DISTRIBUTION & EXPORT
**Goal:** Moving from Digital Brain to Physical Production.

1.  **Review Output:** Read the generated script in the right-hand panel.
    *   *Check:* Does the "Hook" hit hard? Is the "Loop" seamless?
2.  **Export:** Click the **Download DOCX** button.
    *   *Filename:* The system auto-generates a standardized name:
        `[Format]-[Topic]-[Date].docx`
        *(e.g., `NanoShort-Procrastination-2024-02-02.docx`)*
3.  **Archive:** The project is now saved in your `Projects` list (Command Deck).

---

## ⚫ PHASE 5: PRODUCTION (Physical Realm)
**Goal:** Execution.

1.  **Open DOCX:** Use the downloaded file as your **Teleprompter Script** or Director's Sheet.
2.  **Film:** Record the content.
    *   *Focus:* Nail the "Visual Pacing" timestamps in the script.
3.  **Edit:** Use the "Visual Cues" column in the DOCX for B-Roll instructions.
4.  **Upload:** Use the optimized Title & Description from the DOCX for YouTube/TikTok.

---

## 🔄 PHASE 6: CLOSING THE LOOP (Self-Learning)
**Goal:** Teaching CORTEX.

*   *Automatic:* The system monitors performance (via YouTube API).
*   *Manual:* If you find a "hack" that works (e.g., specific hook word), go to **Scanner** -> **Wisdom** and manually add it. It will be used in the next Generation.
