# GENESIS WORKFLOW
> The Optimal Path from Trend Radar to Video Production.

## 1. Discovery (Trend Radar)
- **Goal**: Find high-potential "Outlier" ideas.
- **Action**: Use the **Trend Radar** (Brain Circuit Icon) to scan not just for keywords, but for *angles*.
- **Selection**: Click the "+" on ideas. They turn GREEN (Checkmark) to indicate they are in your basket.
- **Result**: These ideas are saved as "Topics" in your Notebook with status **"Idea"**.

## 2. Incubation (Notebook - Topics)
- **Goal**: Refine the raw idea before committing resources.
- **Location**: Notebook -> "TOPICS" Tab -> "Idea" Column.
- **Actions**:
    - **Edit (Pencil)**: Customize the title or add notes about *why* you picked it.
    - **Research (Brain Circuit)**: If you need more info, click the Brain icon on the tile to deep-dive via the Scanner.

## 3. The Genesis Trigger (Scripting)
- **Goal**: Activate the AI Production Crew.
- **Action**: Click the **Lightning Bolt (Zap)** icon on the card OR Drag the Topic to **"Scripting"**.
- **The Event**: This triggers the **Genesis Engine**.
    - **Blueprints**: A full `blueprint.md` is generated (Hook, Twist, Title Options).
    - **Location**: Files are saved to your Desktop folder: `NeuroCode_Teasers / [Title]`.

## 4. Production Protocol
> **"What do I do now?"**
1.  **Open the Folder**: Go to your Desktop -> `NeuroCode_Teasers`. Open the folder matching your Topic title.
2.  **The Blueprint**: Open `blueprint.md`. This is your script and director's guide.
3.  **Filming**:
    - Move card to **"Filming"**.
    - Record the video using the specific "Hook" and "Twist" from the blueprint.
4.  **Editing (Polishing)**:
    - Move card to **"Polishing"**.
    - Use the generated AI Image/Video prompts (found in `assets.md` or `blueprint.md`) to create B-Roll.
5.  **Upload**:
    - Move card to **"Ready"**.
    - Copy the "Optimized Title" and "Description" from the blueprint to YouTube.

## 5. Review (Data Mirror)
- **Goal**: Close the loop.
- **Action**: Once live, the system tracks performance. Use this data to refine future Trend Radar searches.
